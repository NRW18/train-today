## How the examples are used

- Photos illustrate equipment or exercise types, not the user's own gym. Models,
  adjustments, attachments and safe use can differ; follow the original app guide.
- The file named “Girl doing chest press machine exercise” visually shows a
  padded-arm fly station and is used as a pec-deck apparatus example. Rear-delt
  uses the same apparatus photo, not an instruction to copy the pictured movement;
  not every pec-deck supports reverse fly. Existing guides and warnings still apply.
- The cable-station photo illustrates the equipment used for pushdowns, not the
  pushdown movement itself. Forearm plank, standard pushup and seated leg curl were
  selected to match the app's variants. No lying-curl photo is used for seated curl.
- The two George Stepanek sources and the dumbbell-curl source contain two-frame
  photographs; they are retained intact. No original metadata is shipped in WebP.
- These examples are presentation assets only, not a trained recognition model or
  an evaluation dataset. Existing user-saved thumbnails continue to take priority.
