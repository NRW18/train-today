# แหล่งข้อมูลรองเท้า / KRYVOS shoe source audit

Checked **2026-10-15 (15 October 2026)**. **60 distinct models, 10 brands.** Colors, gender and width variants are consolidated within one model, never counted as extra models.

## Method and limitations

- Prices are Thai **list/regular prices (THB)** shown by the store, not color-specific sale prices, coupons or cart promotions. A linked color may currently cost less. Prices, stock and sizes can change; the app is a dated offline snapshot, not a live stock service.
- Brand Thai stores: ASICS, Nike, adidas, New Balance, Puma, On, Mizuno and HOKA. Brooks and Saucony use the Thai REV RUNNR branded store, a local retailer/distributor domain rather than the global brand domain: [REV company information](https://brooks.rev.co.th/pages/about-us), [Saucony/REV stores](https://saucony.rev.co.th/pages/store-locator).
- Product names, Thai list prices, gender labels and descriptions were read from public product pages/catalogs. Shopify catalog evidence: [HOKA](https://hoka.co.th/products.json?limit=250), [Brooks](https://brooks.rev.co.th/collections/brooks/products.json?limit=250), [Saucony](https://brooks.rev.co.th/collections/saucony/products.json?limit=250), [Mizuno](https://tha.mizuno.com/collections/running/products.json?limit=250). No product images downloaded or hotlinked.
- Only verified gender variants are included. A missing gender means **not verified here**, not that the manufacturer never makes that version. Explicit Unisex listings can appear for either selection. Gender-specific price and product URL are used before the budget test.
- Wide only matches documented Wide products, at their own price/link. A verified Wide-only listing is excluded from regular/narrow results. No Narrow SKUs were verified: choosing Narrow shows an explicit empty result and explanation, rather than silently substituting regular shoes. Normal/unsure arch remains a ranking preference; flat arch requires documented stability support.
- Use categories (daily/cushion/speed/race/trail), stability flag and cushion classes (1 light / 2 medium / 3 high) are editorial classifications of the store descriptions, not laboratory results or clinical advice. Field r is retained but set to 0 throughout: no invented review score. A trail racer is classified as trail, not a road race recommendation.
- New Balance includes some contradictory JSON-LD with zero IDR offers. Its visible Thai data-price-amount and regular oldPrice values were used instead. This is recorded here rather than treating the malformed offer as a free shoe.
- Legacy catalog entries without verified price/product evidence were removed from recommendations. Existing saved shoe names and mileage are independent of this catalog and remain intact, including old backups.
- All six category SVG illustrations are original KRYVOS drawings, not product depictions. They use existing light/dark theme tokens and work offline. Visiting store links needs internet. No new dependency or analytics event is introduced.
- Three Thai Mizuno listings lack detailed descriptions. Their use/cushion classification was cross-checked against the manufacturer's regional product pages, checked on the same date; Thai prices still come exclusively from the Thai listings: [Wave Rider 29](https://emea.mizuno.com/eu/en-gb/wave-rider-29/J1GC266301.html) (moderate cushioning and daily running), [Wave Rebellion Flash 3](https://emea.mizuno.com/eu/en-gb/wave-rebellion-flash-3/J1GD253521.html) (speed training, ENERZY XP/NXT), [Wave Mujin 11](https://emea.mizuno.com/eu/en-nl/wave-mujin-11/J1GJ257052.html) (trail running and cushioning).
